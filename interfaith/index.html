Latter-day Saint Dialogue

The read-along series, led nightly by Trevor

This is the heart of the interfaith work: a Catholic reading Latter-day Saint scripture together with Latter-day Saint friends — hosted and led by Trevor every night. It began with a question — should a Catholic read the Book of Mormon, in public, with charity? It became nightly reading, real friendship, and a community that prays the Rosary together — and a model of how Catholics and Latter-day Saints can meet with honesty and charity. The series continue here, as one part of a larger work.

Interfaith Leadership

Leading the Conversation

Host of the nightly read-along Spaces since June 2026; gathered the community in person at General Conference in Salt Lake City (October 2026); guest on Ward Radio; and co-author of research on Catholic–Latter-day Saint dialogue. Dialogue led with charity — and without blurring real differences.

How It Started

The story so far
  1. June 6, 2026

    A post and a question

    "Stop being dicks to Mormons" — 46,000+ views. Hours later, a question: should a Catholic read the Book of Mormon, in public, with charity? The answer became everything below.

  2. Summer 2026

    Nightly X Spaces read-along

    Night after night, the Book of Mormon read together — opening prayer, Catholic spiritual reading, the chapters, open discussion, and the Rosary to close. A community formed around it.

  3. October 2–4, 2026

    Salt Lake City

    General Conference weekend and an in-person meet-and-greet with the read-along community — online friendship made real.

  4. October 2026

    The Pearl of Great Price

    The next series begins: a Catholic reads the Pearl of Great Price, with the same format and the same charity. Completed October 27, 2026.

  5. October 28, 2026

    The Doctrine and Covenants

    The read-along moves on to the Doctrine and Covenants — a few sections a night at the community's own pace, on toward a finish in January 2027.

  6. November 14, 2026

    Magnifica Humanitas

    The work turns toward sharing the faith itself: a parish session on Pope Leo XIV’s encyclical on AI and human dignity at St. Patrick Church, with the recording, slides, and infographic published for anyone to use.

  7. November 2026

    One home: A Catholic Reads & The WayPoint

    The WayPoint — Trevor’s Catholic resource hub of prayer tools, the Trinity Model, and resources — folds into A Catholic Reads, so the evangelism, the tools, and the reading friendships live under one roof.

A Catholic Reads — an independent project of Dr. Trevor Tomesh for exploring and sharing the Catholic faith.
The reading series are works of friendship and study across traditions; they are not endorsements of other traditions’ doctrines, and other traditions’ scriptures are not recognized as Scripture by the Catholic Church.
