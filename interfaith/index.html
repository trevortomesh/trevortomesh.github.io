How It Started

The story so far
  1. June 6, 2026

    A post and a question

    "Stop being dicks to Mormons" — 46,000+ views. Hours later, a question: should a Catholic read the Book of Mormon, in public, with charity? The answer became everything below.

  2. Summer 2026

    Nightly X Spaces read-along

    Night after night, the Book of Mormon read together — opening prayer, Catholic spiritual reading, the chapters, open discussion, and the Rosary to close. A community formed around it.

  3. October 2–4, 2026

    Salt Lake City

    General Conference weekend and an in-person meet-and-greet with the read-along community — online friendship made real.

  4. October 2026

    The Pearl of Great Price

    The next series begins: a Catholic reads the Pearl of Great Price, with the same format and the same charity.

A Catholic in Dialogue with the Latter-day Saints
This is an independent interfaith project for understanding and dialogue. It is not an endorsement of Latter-day Saint doctrine, and Latter-day Saint scripture is not recognized as Scripture by the Catholic Church.
