About Trevor

Trevor Tomesh was born and raised Catholic. After falling away from the faith for roughly twenty years, he returned to the Catholic Church.

Trevor holds a PhD in Computer Science and is a university professor. His academic background shapes the way he approaches difficult questions: with curiosity, careful analysis, and a willingness to take ideas seriously on their own terms.

A Catholic Reads the Book of Mormon brings that approach into interfaith dialogue. The project is an effort to read the Book of Mormon seriously from a Catholic perspective, to understand what Latter-day Saints actually believe, and to create space for charitable discussion of both common ground and genuine theological differences.

The goal is not to erase those differences. It is to demonstrate that people can disagree about matters of deep religious importance while still listening to one another, praying together where appropriate, asking sincere questions, and treating one another with Christian charity.

A Catholic Reads the Book of Mormon
This is an independent interfaith project presenting a Catholic reading of the Book of Mormon for understanding and dialogue. It is not an endorsement of Latter-day Saint doctrine, and the Book of Mormon is not recognized as Scripture by the Catholic Church.
