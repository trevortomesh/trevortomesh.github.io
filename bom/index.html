Why this project?

Understanding before argument.

This project began as a Catholic professor reading the Book of Mormon with Latter-day Saints and other Christians in live X Spaces. The readings became a place for Scripture, questions, Catholic theology, LDS perspectives, prayer, the Rosary, and—above all—conversation conducted in good faith.

We do not have to pretend our theological differences are small in order to treat one another with charity.

A note from the host

A Catholic reading, not a conversion project.

I am Catholic. I do not accept the Book of Mormon as Catholic sacred Scripture, and this project is not an endorsement of LDS doctrine. It is an attempt to encounter another faith tradition honestly, to explain Catholic belief when questions arise, and to foster friendship and serious interfaith dialogue among people who often disagree about very important things.

The views expressed here are my own and do not necessarily reflect those of my university.
