The Surrender Novena

Nine days of handing everything back to Jesus — your plans, your worries, the things you cannot fix — and resting in His care.

What is the Surrender Novena?

The Surrender Novena comes from Don Dolindo Ruotolo (1882–1970), a priest of Naples, Italy, whose cause for canonization is underway. In 1940, in prayer, Don Dolindo received words he attributed to Jesus — an invitation not to strain, scheme, and worry our way through our troubles, but to abandon ourselves to Him completely. Padre Pio is reported to have sent his own penitents from Naples back home with the words: "Go to Don Dolindo — he is a saint."

The heart of the novena is a single sentence, prayed again and again across nine days. It is not a magic formula and it is not passivity: it is the deliberate, repeated act of entrusting to Jesus the very things your anxiety keeps trying to manage for Him.

"O Jesus, I surrender myself to You, take care of everything!"

The refrain of the novena — prayed ten times each day

How to pray it

  1. Pick your nine days. Pray one day of the novena each day for nine consecutive days, ideally for a particular intention you want to entrust to Jesus.
  2. Read the day's meditation slowly. The short reflections below follow the themes of Don Dolindo's text for each day.
  3. Pray the refrain ten times. After the meditation, say "O Jesus, I surrender myself to You, take care of everything!" ten times — slowly, meaning it a little more each time. The pray-along below counts them with you.
  4. Close with the Hail Mary and the short prayer to Our Lady provided at the end of each day.
  5. Then stop. The discipline of the novena is what you do after you pray: leave the matter with Him instead of picking it back up.

Pray along

Choose a day, read its meditation, then tap the beads (or the "Pray the refrain" button) to count your ten repetitions. Your completed days are remembered on this device.
